Television Host, Media Personality, Producer & Creator

Ashley Singleton is a rising television host, media personality, producer, speaker, and emcee known for her polished on-camera presence, engaging interview style, and ability to connect with people. Her career spans television, film, entertainment, live events, and original programming, with a focus on celebrity interviews and cinematic entertainment.

Ashley currently serves as a co-host on Cedric the Entertainer’s FanRoom Live and previously served as the host and on-camera face of Behind the Scenes Las Vegas (BTSLV). Her entertainment reporting has taken her onto movie sets, red carpets, and major industry events, where she has interviewed actors, musicians, athletes, and entertainment leaders including Terrence Howard, Billy Zane, T-Pain, Ne-Yo, Flavor Flav, Brian Austin Green, O.T. Genasis, Dave Asprey, Malin Akerman, and Michael Jai White.

Television & Entertainment

Ashley’s interview style is polished yet fun. She knows how to keep a conversation engaging while creating space for people to show their personality. Her work includes movie set interviews with cast and production teams, celebrity red carpets, and coverage of productions and events including Redemption, The Hook, Dustlands, WWE, Global Gaming League, Waltzing with Brando, Shaken & Stirred, and Biohack Yourself.

Ashley is drawn to conversations that reveal the person behind the public image. She believes audiences want to know the real person behind the career, the success, and the headlines.

From Event Production to Television

Before entering television and media, Ashley spent 17 years building a career in professional event design, production, and planning. She has produced high-profile experiences for globally recognized brands and organizations including AWS, the NFL, GoPro, the Museum of the Bible, adidas, T-Mobile, and SeriesFest.

Her production background gave her the experience to lead under pressure, manage complex productions, work with major brands, and create experiences that leave an impression. It also gave her a deep understanding of what happens behind the camera and what it takes to bring a production to life.

That experience ultimately led Ashley toward television, where she could combine her production background with her natural ability to communicate and connect with people on camera.

Creator of Seventh Gear

Ashley is the creator, host, and executive producer of Seventh Gear, an original unscripted television series combining celebrity interviews, motorcycles, adventure, human exploration, and cinematic storytelling.

The idea came to Ashley while preparing for a red carpet interview with Keanu Reeves at the Chicano Hollywood Film Festival. As a fellow rider, Ashley began thinking about what it would be like to have a deeper conversation with someone she respected while riding together on a motorcycle.

That idea became Seventh Gear.

The series is designed like a feature film and road movie, following Ashley and notable personalities on motorcycles while exploring the experiences, challenges, and turning points that shaped their lives.

At the heart of Seventh Gear is a philosophy of resilience: sometimes life demands one more gear.

For Ashley, motorcycles represent freedom, peace, adventure, and joy. They create an environment where people can relax, open up, and have a real conversation. Seventh Gear brings that experience to television through a format that combines motorcycles, celebrity interviews, music, travel, and human stories.

The first episode is currently in production, with Ashley continuing to develop the series for television, studios, networks, and distribution partners.

A Rising Television Host

Ashley’s career is driven by three things:

Curiosity fuels ideas. Connection elevates people. Authenticity is the advantage.

She brings preparation, personality, and professionalism to every project while staying true to her own voice. Her goal is to build a lasting career in television and create programs that audiences genuinely want to watch.

As she continues growing as a television host, producer, and creator, Ashley is pursuing opportunities that challenge her, expand her reach, and allow her to tell compelling stories in her own way.
