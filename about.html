Television Host, Media Personality, Producer & Creator

Ashley Singleton is a rising television host, media personality, producer, speaker, and emcee known for her polished on-camera presence, engaging interview style, and ability to connect with people. Her career spans television, film, entertainment, live events, and original programming, with a focus on celebrity interviews and cinematic entertainment.

Currently a co-host on Cedric the Entertainer’s FanRoom Live, she previously was the host and on-camera face of Behind the Scenes Las Vegas (BTSLV). Her entertainment reporting has taken her onto movie sets, red carpets, and major industry events, where she has interviewed actors, musicians, athletes, and entertainment leaders including Terrence Howard, Billy Zane, T-Pain, Ne-Yo, Flavor Flav, Brian Austin Green, O.T. Genasis, Dave Asprey, Malin Akerman, and Michael Jai White.

Television & Entertainment

Her interview style is polished yet fun, combining professionalism with a genuine interest in the people she meets. Her work includes movie set interviews with cast and production teams, celebrity red carpets, and coverage of productions and events including Redemption, The Hook, Dustlands, WWE, Global Gaming League, Waltzing with Brando, Shaken & Stirred, and Biohack Yourself.

She is drawn to conversations that reveal the person behind the public image, giving audiences a chance to see a different side of familiar faces beyond the career, success, and headlines.

From Event Production to Television

Before entering television and media, she spent 17 years building a career in professional event design, production, and planning. Her work includes high-profile experiences for globally recognized brands and organizations such as AWS, the NFL, GoPro, the Museum of the Bible, adidas, T-Mobile, and SeriesFest.

That background provided a strong foundation in production, live entertainment, brand experiences, and leadership. Working behind the scenes meant learning how to operate under pressure, manage complex productions, collaborate with teams, and deliver experiences that leave an impression.

The move into television brought those skills together with a natural ability to communicate and connect with people on camera.

Creator of Seventh Gear

She is the creator, host, and executive producer of Seventh Gear, an original unscripted television series combining celebrity interviews, motorcycles, adventure, human exploration, and cinematic storytelling.

The idea came while preparing for a red carpet interview with Keanu Reeves at the Chicano Hollywood Film Festival. As a fellow rider, she began thinking about what it would be like to have a deeper conversation with someone she respected while riding together on a motorcycle.

That idea became Seventh Gear.

Designed with the visual language of a feature film and road movie, the series follows her and notable personalities on motorcycles while exploring the experiences, challenges, and turning points that shaped their lives.

At the heart of Seventh Gear is a philosophy of resilience: sometimes life demands one more gear.

For her, motorcycles represent freedom, peace, adventure, and joy. They create an environment where people can relax, open up, and have a real conversation. The series brings that experience to television through a format combining motorcycles, celebrity interviews, music, travel, and human stories.

The first episode is currently in production, with the series being developed for television, studios, networks, and distribution partners.

A Rising Television Host

Her career is guided by three simple principles:

Curiosity fuels ideas. Connection elevates people. Authenticity is the advantage.

Preparation, personality, and professionalism shape the way she approaches every project. The goal is to build a lasting career in television while creating programs that audiences genuinely want to watch.

As her work continues to expand across television, film, and original programming, she is pursuing opportunities that challenge her, expand her reach, and allow her to create meaningful work in her own voice.
