I hosted the red carpet coverage for the OPTIX premiere of Waltzing with Brando at SAHARA Las Vegas, interviewing the filmmakers and cast as they celebrated the release of the biographical drama.

Starring Billy Zane as Marlon Brando, the film explores the actor’s friendship with architect Bernard Judge, played by Jon Heder, and their vision to build a sustainable retreat in Tahiti. The cast also includes Richard Dreyfuss, Tia Carrere, Rob Corddry, Camille Razat, Alaina Huffman, and James Jagger.

The evening gave me the opportunity to share exclusive conversations with the talent behind the film while giving audiences a closer look at one of Las Vegas’ standout independent film premieres.
